WLOS August 5, 2026 Monitor

Independent Monitor: HCA Cannot Sell Mission for 10 Years — and Potential Noncompliance Stands

Following the August 4 community Q&A webinar, WLOS reported that independent monitor representative Rachel Ryan confirmed HCA is contractually barred from selling Mission Hospital without advisory board consent during the first 10 years of the Asset Purchase Agreement. Ryan also restated the 2025 monitoring report's findings of potential noncompliance on service discontinuation and multiple Immediate Jeopardy designations — noncompliance that has now been flagged for the third consecutive year.

How to position this
  • The APA's no-sale obligation does not equal accountability for care — HCA remains bound to run Mission safely, and the monitor has flagged noncompliance for three years running.
  • Potential noncompliance on service discontinuation means WNC patients have lost access to care HCA promised to maintain when it bought a nonprofit hospital for $1.5 billion.
  • Four Immediate Jeopardy citations since 2019 are themselves a basis for potential noncompliance — the monitor's own report says so.
  • Ask HCA: if Mission is truly in compliance, why has the independent monitor flagged concerns every year and why has the AG's lawsuit survived summary judgment?

On August 5, WLOS reported on the independent monitor's public Q&A webinar held August 4. Monitor rep Rachel Ryan clarified that under the Asset Purchase Agreement, HCA is obligated not to sell Mission Hospital during the first 10 years without advisory board consent, and said she was unaware of any HCA intent to sell. Ryan also restated the 2025 report's findings of potential noncompliance related to service discontinuation and Mission's string of Immediate Jeopardy designations since the HCA acquisition. Over 90 community members attended the webinar hosted by Dogwood Health Trust and Affiliated Monitors.

Judge Denies HCA Summary Judgment; AG Jackson's Mission Hospital Lawsuit Proceeds

NC Business Court Judge Julianna Earp largely denied HCA Healthcare's motion for summary judgment on July 28, ruling in favor of several of AG Jeff Jackson's arguments. The lawsuit, which alleges HCA failed to maintain the emergency and cancer care it promised when it acquired Mission Health in 2019, will now proceed. AG Jackson said: the case moves forward and he will keep fighting for emergency and cancer care in western NC.

On July 28, 2026, NC Business Court Judge Julianna Earp largely denied HCA Healthcare's motion for summary judgment in Attorney General Jeff Jackson's lawsuit over Mission Hospital care commitments. The AG's office said the ruling rejected HCA's attempts to have the case thrown out and upheld several of the state's arguments. The lawsuit centers on allegations that HCA failed to maintain the levels of emergency and oncology services it committed to when purchasing the nonprofit Mission Health System in 2019 for $1.5 billion. 2019
Acquisition
HCA Healthcare, a Fortune 100 for-profit hospital corporation, acquires Mission Health for $1.5 billion, promising to maintain staffing levels, services, and investment in WNC. The NC Attorney General's office extracts legally binding commitments as a condition of approval.
2019–2022
The exodus
More than 200 physicians and 600 nurses leave Mission. Staff-to-bed ratios fall from 6.1 FTE to 3.7 FTE — well below the 5.1 NC average. Patient experience scores drop to 2 stars and stay there. Mission receives its first Immediate Jeopardy citation in 2021.
2023–2024
Sanctions escalate
Blue Ridge Regional Hospital (HCA-owned, Spruce Pine) cited for Immediate Jeopardy in 2023. Mission receives a second and third Immediate Jeopardy citation in 2024–2025. CMS issues an Enhanced Plan of Correction. Reclaim Healthcare WNC formalizes as a 501(c)(3) coalition of physicians, nurses, elected officials, and community members.
Jan 2026
Fourth IJ
Mission is placed in Immediate Jeopardy for the fourth time — three times in less than two years. CEO tells employees via internal message. National Nurses United responds publicly, calling conditions unsafe. HCA's earnings language simultaneously describes "cost discipline" and margin improvement.
Apr 2026
CON awarded
NC DHHS awards Mission 95 new hospital beds — despite active federal compliance orders and the lowest patient experience scores of any CON applicant. Reclaim testifies in opposition, presenting HCAHPS data showing Mission at 2 stars vs. Advent Health at 4 stars every year since 2020.
